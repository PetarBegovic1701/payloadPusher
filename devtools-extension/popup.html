Payload Capture

This extension works inside DevTools, not from this button.

  1. Open the page of your app.
  2. Open DevTools: F12, or Ctrl+Shift+I (Cmd+Opt+I on a Mac).
  3. Click the Payload Capture tab. If you do not see it, look in the » menu.

DevTools was already open when you loaded the extension? Close DevTools and open it again.
